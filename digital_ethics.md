💻 Digital Ethics

What Is Digital Ethics?

Digital ethics refers to the principles and values that guide responsible and respectful behaviour when using digital technologies.

Important Principles

1. Respect Privacy

Do not access or share another person's private information without permission.

2. Respect Copyright

Give proper credit to creators and avoid copying their work without permission.

3. Be Respectful Online

Avoid cyberbullying, harassment, and spreading harmful content.

4. Share Information Responsibly

Check whether information is reliable before sharing it online.

5. Use Technology Responsibly

Use digital tools in a lawful, ethical, and responsible manner.

Why Is Digital Ethics Important?

Digital ethics promotes trust, privacy, fairness, respect, and responsible behaviour in the digital world.

Conclusion

Being a responsible digital citizen means using technology safely, honestly, and respectfully.
