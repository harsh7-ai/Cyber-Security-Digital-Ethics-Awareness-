🔐 What Is Cyber Security?

Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and personal information from digital attacks and unauthorized access.

Common Cyber Threats

1. Phishing

Phishing is a fraudulent attempt to trick people into sharing sensitive information through fake emails, messages, or websites.

2. Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage devices, disrupt operations, or access information without permission.

3. Identity Theft

Identity theft occurs when someone misuses another person's personal information without authorization.

4. Online Scams

Online scams use deception to trick people into sharing information or losing money.

How to Stay Safe Online

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable two-factor authentication.
- Never share OTPs or passwords.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links.
- Keep software updated.
- Download applications from trusted sources.

Conclusion

Cyber security helps protect our digital identities, devices, and information. Everyone should follow safe online practices.
